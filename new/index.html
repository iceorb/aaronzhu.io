01About

I’m an engineer and researcher in New York, currently working at Mercor.

I’ve done applied research at DoorDash and TikTok. Before that, research at the Safe Foundational Models Lab (Johns Hopkins/UW-Madison) and the Vision & Learning Lab (Yonsei University).

My favorite book recently (or rather, novella) is The Lifecycle of Software Objects by Ted Chiang.

I like to travel, snowboard, play tennis, and pick up languages.
